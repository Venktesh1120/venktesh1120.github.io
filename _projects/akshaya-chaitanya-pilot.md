## Problem
A new pilot program needed a validated feature set and roadmap, but requirements were scattered across
160+ stakeholders with no structured way to prioritise what to build first.

## Approach
Ran structured primary research across all 160+ stakeholders to surface and validate requirements, then
translated findings into BRD-aligned feature specs and a phased execution plan.

## What I did
- Conducted structured stakeholder research to gather and validate product requirements
- Built and managed an end-to-end product roadmap with defined milestones over an 8-week program
- Designed and ran A/B tests on pilot features to validate engagement hypotheses
- Used User Story Mapping to align cross-functional teams on scope and enable iterative delivery
- Built automated KPI dashboards in Google Analytics and Excel to replace ad-hoc reporting

## Results
- Improved conversion rates across key user flows from data-backed iteration
- 100% on-time delivery of all programme outputs across the 8-week cycle
- Reduced leadership reporting time via reusable performance tracking frameworks
