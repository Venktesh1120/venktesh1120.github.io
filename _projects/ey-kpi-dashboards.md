## Problem
Client stakeholders across 10+ global engagements lacked real-time visibility into business performance,
relying on manual, ad-hoc reporting that slowed decision-making.

## Approach
Conducted market research and technology assessments to understand client needs, then partnered with
business stakeholders and engineering teams to translate reporting gaps into a scalable dashboard solution.

## What I did
- Designed and built Power BI and Tableau dashboards using SQL and Python for real-time KPI tracking
- Ran gap analyses and solution benchmarking to guide tooling and vendor decisions
- Delivered training programs to drive adoption of new reporting workflows across teams

## Results
- 40% improvement in reporting efficiency
- 40% reduction in user errors through simplified workflows
